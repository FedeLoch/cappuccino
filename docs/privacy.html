Privacy policy

Cappuccino doesn't collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • It doesn't connect to the internet.
  • Your artworks, references, and settings stay on your device, in the app's own storage.
  • The companion's feedback comes from image analysis that runs entirely on your device. Your art is never uploaded or used to train anything.
  • Files leave the app only when you export or share them, and they go where you choose.

If you uninstall the app, Android deletes everything it stored. Exported files stay where you saved them.

Questions: write to the contact email shown on the Google Play listing.
